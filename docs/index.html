Does encoding a biological question as language buy generalization to questions the model was never trained on? If yes, one model answers a family of typed biological questions, and a new question is a new sentence rather than a new training run. This site is every experiment run to find out — including the ones that failed, and the two controls that turned out to be broken.

The instruments work. The biology is not there yet.
The text encoder has stopped being a lookup table, which was v0.1's fatal flaw. But against a 20-dimensional bag of amino acids — the only baseline that counts — the model still loses on four of five held-out pathogens, and a version that never reads the question at all scores 0.533 against the full model's 0.555. Nothing here is a biological claim yet, and every page below says so.

The work

In the order it happened

Decisive The negative result
v0.1. Trained on three peptide properties, the text encoder scored 0.715 against a three-row lookup table's 0.718. Three questions in a 384-dimensional space is two degrees of freedom, so there was nothing compositional to learn.
Half a resolution The v0.2 benchmark
21 pathogen assays, mechanistic prompts, cross-attention. The lookup equivalence breaks (+0.054). Against the composition ceiling — the zero that matters — the mean lift is −0.058 and four of five held-out targets lose to a bag of amino acids.
Resolved v0.3: the fourth outcome
Matching removed the shortcut and the signal with it: 72% of sequences carry both labels, so the 0.51 ceiling is real. Pre-registered outcome four. A scrambled-decoy control then clears 0.86 over a 0.5 ceiling, so the fine-tuning path itself is sound.
16/16 The control rig
The evaluation tested on synthetic data whose ground truth we control, in a regime where transfer is possible and one where it is impossible by construction. Includes the correction to the entity_only control, which was measuring a label prior.

Experiments completed

Every run so far, and what each one settled

Experiment Verdict What it settled
Arc virtual cell sample Unusable 99.94% of labels are “no change” and 5 target genes means 5 questions. Plumbing test only.
Synthetic control rig Passed 16/16 pre-registered checks. The harness transfers when transfer is possible (+0.96) and not when it isn't (−0.09). The instrument works.
Entity-only shortcut discovery Expectation was wrong A pre-registered expectation failed at +0.204. Shortcut level is now measured per split instead of assumed.
v0.3 matched negatives (650M, unfrozen) Unlearnable 0.514 against a 0.515 ceiling, validation loss pinned at ln 2, drift 7.8e-05. 72% of sequences carry both labels and 100% of negatives are confirmed broad-spectrum AMPs, so ~0.51 is the true ceiling. Pre-registered outcome four.
Scrambled-decoy positive control Passed Composition ceiling 0.5 by construction. Live models reach 0.854–0.860; unfreezing two blocks is worth +0.052 ± 0.003 across three seeds. The fine-tuning path works — the null was the benchmark, not the code.
PeptiVerse, 3 tasks (v0.1) Decisive dual ≈ task_id. Three questions in a 384-d space are 2 degrees of freedom; the text encoder became an expensive one-hot.
AMP multi-task, 21 assays (v0.2) Half a resolution Lookup floor cleared (+0.054). Composition ceiling not cleared (mean −0.058, 1/5 targets above, and that one inside the intervals).
Ceiling re-derivation Correction The v0.2 ceilings were fitted in sample, inflating them by 0.012–0.039. Now scored out of fold, in one shared module.
AMP answer-label semantics Correction The AMP loader labelled rows by activity (1 = reported active) but placed the active answer at option index 0, and cross-entropy reads those labels as option indices — so every answer-reading model was trained to prefer the wrong answer text. Answers now follow the labels, runs record label_semantics_version, and regression tests trace source activity through the training tensors under all three negative policies with balancing on and off. No stored AMP report carries that field, so no published AMP number survives as evidence about answer semantics.
Audit of the controls themselves Two were broken The cross-attention model never read the candidate answers — it classified into fixed slots, so the answer text was unused and the option count was stuck at two. And entity_only, the control that is supposed to detect “answers without reading the question”, scored every option identically, so it emitted one answer for every row: it was measuring the label prior. On the split where the rig famously caught a shortcut at 0.602, “always answer option 0” scores exactly 0.6021. Both fixed; the rig still passes 16/16, and the repaired control raises the measured ceiling on one split by 0.13.
Resolved · Aster v0.3

Make the shortcut impossible, and the signal goes with it

Design frozen · run executed · pre-registered outcome four landed

Resolved, 28 September 2026. The comparison was re-run under label semantics v2 and the fourth pre-registered outcome is the one that landed: everything collapses to chance, and the dataset cannot test the premise. The earlier runs with inverted option indices remain in reports/ as history and are not results.

Every number on this site is measured against a ceiling high enough to swallow the effect being measured: positives are real antimicrobial peptides, negatives are peptides with no record, and the two pools differ in bulk composition. So “is this peptide cationic and amphipathic?” answered most of the benchmark without reading the question — on C. albicans, to 0.721.

Matching each positive to its nearest negative in composition-and-length space drove that ceiling to chance by construction, 0.721 → 0.522 on the fungal target. It also drove the signal to chance. ESM-2 650M with two blocks unfrozen scored 0.514 against a 0.515 ceiling, with validation loss pinned at ln 2 and encoder drift of 7.8e-05. The cause is measurable rather than mysterious: 72% of sequences in the matched benchmark carry both labels, and 100% of the presumed negatives are confirmed broad-spectrum antimicrobial peptides. The encoder sees one sequence with two opposite answers, and the only differing input is a prompt the swap ablation already showed to be a task identifier. Matching on composition was matching on the discriminator.

A null cannot tell a bad task from bad code, and that difference decides whether more compute is worth buying — so a scrambled-decoy positive control was added: negatives become order-shuffled copies of the positives, identical in composition and length, with motif and helicity destroyed. Both ceilings are then 0.5 by construction. Live models reach 0.854–0.860, and unfreezing two blocks is worth +0.052 ± 0.003 across three seeds while task_id and question_only stay at chance. The fine-tuning path works. The null belonged to the benchmark.

What that control deliberately does not settle: with scrambled decoys the answer does not depend on the pathogen, so entity_only matching dual is the expected outcome and the mechanistic-prompt hypothesis remains untested. The blocker is now data rather than capacity. Scaling to a larger ESM on presumed negatives reproduces a null at higher cost, because the ceiling is a property of the labels — the next experiment worth running is the one that acquires negatives somebody actually measured.

Experiments of the future

Queued, in the order they would change the most

01 Queued Data with measured inactives
Every negative in this project is presumed: the source tables record reported activity and never recorded inactivity, so a negative means “no record”, which is partly a statement about what anyone assayed. Matching removes a confound; it does not create a measured negative. The clean answer needs MIC tables with upper bounds rather than lists of hits.
02 Queued Seed variance and split variance
Every number published so far is one seed on one split, with binomial intervals that ignore peptide-level correlation. Before any lift is called real it needs to survive re-drawing both, and the held-out targets need to rotate rather than stay fixed at five.
03 Queued Does a larger protein backbone clear the ceiling?
Everything here runs on ESM-2 8M. If matched negatives leave only residue-order signal, backbone capacity becomes the binding constraint, and 650M is the obvious next rung. Cheap to test, and it cleanly separates “the premise is wrong” from “the encoder is too small.”
04 Queued Calibration and abstention under shift
On unseen questions the v0.1 models were confidently wrong — expected calibration error of 0.22 to 0.32, overconfidence positive everywhere. For a model whose purpose is deciding what to test next, that is the failure that matters most. An abstention rule has to be validated under distribution shift; entropy alone is not evidence that a model knows when it is wrong.
05 Queued Back to cells, with a real perturbation cohort
The original first capability — will reducing one gene's activity raise or lower another gene's RNA? — is still untested, because the 600-cell Arc sample is 99.94% one label. It needs the full atlas or the filtered Replogle set, both of which need a licensing and provenance audit before a single training run.
06 Queued Conjunctive questions, where the data allows
Compositional questions are what made the synthetic regime transfer, and PeptiVerse could not supply them: task overlap is 186 peptides for hemolysis and penetrance, and 2 for hemolysis and solubility. The multi-pathogen data has the overlap that PeptiVerse lacked, so conjunctions across targets are now formable — and they are the sharpest available test of whether the text tower composes rather than memorizes.
